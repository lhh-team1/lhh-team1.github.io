Team Riptide made Wrinkles Reef Rescue, a game to go with Project B.E.A.C.O.N.
Swim Wrinkles around the reef under the buoy, collect gems, and don't get caught.

## [Play Wrinkles Reef Rescue](/Wrinkles.html)

## How to play

- **Arrow keys** or **WASD** to swim
- **Spacebar** for a CCA Boost
- Ride the sound waves coming from the buoy for an Acoustic Boost
- Watch out for Collin the Crab. He just might eat you!

You need a keyboard to play, so use a computer or Chromebook, not a phone.
