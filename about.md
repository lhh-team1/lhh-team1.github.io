## This season

Our Innovation Project is Project B.E.A.C.O.N., which stands for Broadcast
Emitter of Ambient Coral Ocean Noise. It's a buoy that plays healthy reef sounds
and releases coralline algae to bring coral larvae back to damaged reefs. We
pitched it to a marine biologist, who helped us build a working prototype.

Our robot is named Walter. We built it without instructions, and we program it
with Pybricks.

We also made a game, [Wrinkles Reef Rescue](/Wrinkles.html).

To learn more, see our [project design](/design/), our
[business proposal](/2026/10/20/business-proposal-project-beacon/), and our
[research repository](/2026/10/28/research-repository/).
